# FiscalScope — Immersive Storyboard

## Running publication system

A thin running header shows the masthead, current chapter and fiscal year. A numbered
chapter rail replaces conventional website navigation on large screens; `ÍNDICE` opens a
compact chapter sheet on small screens. Figures carry `FIG.`, sources carry `FUENTE`, and
technical notes carry `NOTA`.

The page alternates quiet paper, dense figures and one inverted chapter. It does not
repeat `title → description → card → chart`.

---

## SCENE 01 — The live cover

**Frame:** `FISCALSCOPE / INTERACTIVE FISCAL PAPER / ESPAÑA 2012—2026` shares the
viewport with the salary input and live net result.

**Action:** Dragging the salary scale or changing year immediately moves the net number
and recomposes the 100-unit field.

**Exit line:** `DE CADA 100 € BRUTOS, X € LLEGAN A TU CUENTA.`

**Rhythm:** Quiet, typographic, almost no container chrome.

---

## SCENE 02 — What remains

**Frame:** The 100-unit salary composition expands to full width. Direct labels attach to
net, IRPF and worker Social Security.

**Action:** Hover/focus previews; click pins and carries that category into the next scene.
Zoom reveals block-level detail on small screens.

**Exit line:** `NO ES UNA TARTA. ES TU SUELDO, CONTADO EURO A EURO.`

---

## SCENE 03 — The journey of one salary

**Frame:** The paper turns charcoal. A single proportional stream begins at employer cost.

**Scroll sequence:**

1. Total employer cost appears.
2. Employer contribution peels away.
3. Gross salary remains.
4. Worker contribution peels away.
5. IRPF peels away.
6. Net income remains as the final stream.

**Action:** Stage hover/focus and click-to-pin; zoom/pan; linked component highlight.

**Exit line:** `CUESTA X €. LLEGAN Y €.`

---

## SCENE 04 — Progressive taxation, physically explained

**Frame:** A full-width income ruler is divided proportionally into the legal brackets.

**Action:** The user's taxable income fills each bracket and stops inside the active one.
Hover/focus isolates a slice; click pins; zoom exposes narrow ranges.

**Annotation:** `TU RENTA TERMINA AQUÍ` and exact tax generated by every occupied slice.

**Exit line:** `EL ÚLTIMO TRAMO NO REESCRIBE LOS ANTERIORES.`

---

## SCENE 05 — The memorable distinction

**Frame:** A quiet typographic interruption.

`35 % / NO ES / TU TIPO EFECTIVO.`

The marginal slice and whole-base effective strip sit beside the statement.

**Action:** Hovering either term highlights the matching denominator in Scene 04.

---

## SCENE 06 — Historical atlas

**Opening frame:** A paired comparison answers what the same real salary yielded in the
pinned year and in 2026.

**Main frame:** A nearly full-bleed direct-labelled curve atlas. Current is ink, pinned is
coral, context is gray.

**Action:** Year scrubber, click-to-pin, keyboard arrows, salary-domain brush and zoom.
The comparison number, effective-rate scene and event annotation update together.

**Margin events:** Short 2015, 2019 and 2026 reform notes sit on hairlines, not bands.

---

## SCENE 07 — Your historical trace

**Frame:** Net income is the strong trajectory. Effective IRPF and total worker burden are
secondary aligned traces.

**Action:** The same year scrubber controls all traces. A regime small-multiple strip lets
the reader promote selected years without a rainbow legend.

**Exit line:** `HOY RECIBES X € MÁS/MENOS QUE EN 2019.`

---

## SCENE 08 — Where Spain sits

**Frame:** Salary distribution opens as a social-scale essay: a large density silhouette,
the user's marker and a paired origin/comparison reading.

**Action:** Year pair, salary scrubber and chart zoom. Origin remains ink; comparison uses
the one signal color.

**Following frame:** OECD becomes a long 100%-composition atlas, direct-labelled for Spain
and the OECD mean. Mobile keeps readable labels and allows zoom/pan.

---

## SCENE 09 — The tax wedge

**Frame:** A second inverted rhythm is avoided; the primary dark chapter remains the
salary journey. The tax wedge instead uses a strong ink field on paper unless the journey
and wedge are merged into one continuous chapter.

**Graphic:** One hundred literal blocks of employer cost.

**Action:** Worker/employer perspective morphs the same blocks. Category pinning carries
across the ledger and salary journey. Zoom/pan is available.

**Exit line:** `DE CADA 100 €, X € LLEGAN COMO RENTA NETA.`

---

## SCENE 10 — The hidden mechanism

**Frame:** Art.20 is drawn as one unmistakable shape: `PLANA → CLIFF → CERO`.

**Action:** Salary marker, selected-year scrub, contextual years, zone pinning and
income-domain zoom.

**Second frame:** Threshold evolution becomes a band/range plot. The Art.20 exposure zone
is a band; SMI and withholding exemption are direct-labelled lines.

**Exit line:** `DENTRO DEL CLIFF, 100 € BRUTOS PUEDEN DEJAR SOLO X € NETOS.`

---

## SCENE 11 — Appendix, not footer

**Frame:** `APPENDIX` introduces four paper-like subsections:

- A — Methodology
- B — Sources
- C — Parameters
- D — Normative chronology

The reform almanac retains filters and deep legal links. Figures keep their own source
captions so the appendix verifies rather than rescues them.

---

## Responsive storyboard

### 375px

The running header becomes masthead + active chapter + index. The cover is vertical. Wide
figures retain ambition through deliberate vertical variants or zoomable horizontal
canvases. Hover-only information is always available through focus/click. No body-level
horizontal overflow is allowed.

### 768px

The masthead stays compact; figures use broad single columns. The chapter index is a
sheet. Salary flow and bracket ruler use their desktop direction when labels remain
comfortable, otherwise their mobile variants.

### 1440px+

Margin notes, chapter rail and asymmetric full-width figures become active. Sticky
explanations never obscure controls or sources. Reading text remains narrow while figures
can approach the viewport edges.
